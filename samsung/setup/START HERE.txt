couch-tv for Samsung TVs, version {VERSION}
==========================================

This puts couch-tv on a Samsung TV (2018 or newer) from a Mac or a Windows computer.
It takes about 30 minutes. It is free. You do not need an AI agent or any paid plan.

You need:
- the Samsung TV and a computer on the same Wi-Fi
- a Samsung account (free; the one on your TV is fine)
- the TV's remote, or the free SmartThings app on your phone

What you get: couch-tv as an app on the TV, with couch-tv Studio (designs from your phone).
It is an app, not a new home screen: Samsung's Home button still opens Samsung's menu.


STEP 1. Unzip this folder
-------------------------
Mac: double-click the zip. Windows: right-click the zip, Extract All, Extract.
Use the unzipped folder, not the zip.


STEP 2. Start the setup on the computer
------------------------------------
Mac: double-click "Install on Mac.command".
     If the Mac says it "cannot be opened" or "not verified": click Done, open System Settings,
     Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to "Install on Mac", then Open.
     If the Mac asks to let Terminal find devices on your local network: click Allow.
Windows: double-click "Install on Windows.cmd".
     If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
     If Windows Firewall asks about apps2samsung-cli: tick Private networks and click Allow.

It shows "This computer's address is", like 192.0.2.20. Leave the window open: you type that
address on the TV in step 3.
(No address shown? Mac: System Settings, Wi-Fi, Details next to your network, TCP/IP, "IP address".
Windows: Settings, Network & internet, Wi-Fi, your network's Properties, "IPv4 address".)


STEP 3. Developer mode on the TV
--------------------------------
1. On the TV remote press Home, then open Apps.
2. On the Apps screen, enter 1 2 3 4 5:
   - A remote with number buttons: press 1 2 3 4 5.
   - A slim remote with no numbers (The Frame and most 2021 and newer): press the button
     marked 123 (on some, the button with four coloured dots). Numbers show on screen: pick
     1, 2, 3, 4, 5 with the arrows and OK.
   - No 123 button either: on your phone, open the SmartThings app, pick the TV, open its
     remote, and use its 123 number pad.
   - 2022 and newer TVs: if nothing happens, scroll to the bottom of Apps, open
     App Settings, and enter 1 2 3 4 5 there.
3. A Developer mode box opens. Switch Developer mode to On.
4. Type the address the setup showed in step 2 and press OK. Type each number as it is:
   1 is 1, not 001. Press Right to move to the next group of numbers.
5. Restart the TV: hold the power button down for about 5 seconds, until the TV goes off and
   comes back on. (On The Frame a short press only goes to Art Mode. Hold it.)
   A "Developer mode" line at the top of the Apps screen means it worked.


STEP 4. Finish the setup on the computer
----------------------------------------
In the setup window press Return (Windows: Enter). The setup then:
- downloads Apps2Samsung's free command line tool (open source, about 35 MB, checked before
  it runs). It is what signs and installs Samsung apps.
- finds your TV and checks Developer mode. If something is not right, it says what to fix:
  fix it on the TV, then press Return (Windows: Enter) again.
- opens a browser for your Samsung account sign-in. This is Samsung's own page: couch-tv and
  the setup never see your password. It makes a certificate for your TV only. If Samsung asks
  you to agree or to allow access, say yes, then come back to the setup window.
  Apps2Samsung keeps that signing certificate on this computer, so the next update is signed
  the same way. Keep this computer for updates, and do not share the certificate.
- puts couch-tv on the TV and opens it. Press Allow on the TV if it asks. (If it is not on
  screen, open Couch TV from the TV's row of apps.)


STEP 5. So Couch TV opens again when you switch the TV on
---------------------------------------------------------
On the TV: Settings, General, Smart Features (or Start Screen Option), Autorun Last App: On.
Then, when you switch the TV on, Couch TV is back if it was the last app you used.
To open Couch TV any time: press Home on the Samsung remote, move to Couch TV in the row of
apps, press OK. (Samsung's Home button itself always opens Samsung's menu.)


STEP 6. Get a design from couch-tv Studio
-----------------------------------------
On the TV, open the gear (Home settings), then Designs, then Get a design from your phone.
Scan the QR code with your phone, pick your design and press Send to my TV. Say yes to
Use this design?


Updates
-------
couch-tv on a Samsung does not update itself. When a new one is out, the TV says so on the
home. Then download the newest "couch-tv-samsung-setup.zip" from
github.com/Seagey/couch-tv/releases and run it again (steps 2 and 4). Your settings stay.
Developer mode can switch itself off after a Samsung software update: then do step 3 again.

The setup cannot find the TV? Switch the TV on with its remote first (on The Frame, a short
press: it falls asleep by itself). To keep it reachable: Settings, General, Network, Expert
Settings, Power On with Mobile, On.


Want more? (optional)
---------------------
Couch TV opening by itself every time the TV comes on (even when it was not the last app),
and the phone remote, need a computer at home that stays on (the bridge). That part is in the full guide: docs/SAMSUNG.md on
github.com/Seagey/couch-tv. An AI agent such as Claude Code (it needs a paid Claude plan; any coding
AI agent works) can set it up for you, but you do not need one for anything above.

Stuck? couchtv.joshuaseage.com/help/tv-samsung

About Apps2Samsung
------------------
The setup downloads Apps2Samsung's command line tool, version 3.3.1, from its own GitHub
releases (github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung) and checks its SHA-256 fingerprint before it
runs. Apps2Samsung is a free community tool, not Samsung's, licensed under the GNU General
Public License version 3 (GPL-3.0). couch-tv does not include or change it. Its source code
and licence are on that GitHub page.

To remove couch-tv: on the TV, Apps, the cog (Settings), Couch TV, Delete.
To switch Developer mode off: Apps, 1 2 3 4 5, Developer mode Off, then restart the TV.
