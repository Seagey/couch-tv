      Newer TVs (Google TV, Android 11 or newer)

      1. On the TV: Settings, System, About. Press OK on Android TV OS build (on some TVs, Build) seven times. This shows a hidden menu called Developer options. (Older Android TV: Settings, Device Preferences, About.)
      2. In Developer options, switch on Wireless debugging. This lets Couch TV press keys on this TV. When the TV asks "Allow wireless debugging on this network?", tick Always allow on this network and choose Allow. The TV must be on Wi-Fi.
      3. Careful: Revoke USB debugging authorizations sits right next to Wireless debugging in that list. Never press OK on it: it would undo this setup.
      4. In Wireless debugging choose Pair device with pairing code. Leave that screen open on the TV.
      5. Type the 6 digit code from that screen here. You can leave the port number empty: Couch TV finds it.

      Older TVs (Fire TV, Shield, older Android TV)

      In Developer options, switch on Network debugging (on some TVs, USB debugging). This lets Couch TV press keys on this TV. Then press Let the phone press keys. On the TV, tick Always allow and press Allow. Some newer Google TVs never ask: use the newer TVs steps instead.

      Kids mode (optional, off unless you say yes)

      Any young kids who should only see shows you've picked? Their TV home then shows only the shows you add, and leaving it needs a PIN. Skip this and nothing changes. Once it is on, change it in the phone remote: Kids mode.

      Xiaomi: Couch TV in PatchWall's place (optional, off by default)

      The remote's PatchWall button opens Couch TV, PatchWall opens Couch TV instead, and Couch TV opens when the TV is switched on, unless the TV is going back to an HDMI input. The Home button is not changed. It needs the Home button version of Couch TV (couch-tv-ontv-watch.apk), put on from a computer (the steps are on the help page: couchtv.joshuaseage.com/help/home-button), switched on in the TV's Settings, Accessibility, Couch TV home watch; with this on, that watch also checks each remote key for the PatchWall button and passes every other key on.

      HDMI tiles on the home

      A set-top box, camera or games console plugged into the TV (HDMI 1, HDMI 2 and so on) can have its own tile in the apps row, next to Settings. OK on the tile switches the TV to it. Tick the ones you use and name them. Up to 6. Each one takes an app's place, so your last apps move to More apps.
