# Privacy clean list (Beta). Read by apply.sh when PRIVACY=1. See docs/PRIVACY.md.
#
# Known tracking, telemetry, advertising and ACR (automatic content recognition) apps on Google TV,
# Android TV and Fire TV. apply.sh switches off only the ones a TV actually has, with
# `pm disable-user` (undo.sh switches them back on with `pm enable`), and only after the same safety
# check as EXTRA_OFF: a home screen, a setup app, a persistent process or a core part of Android is
# always left on, whatever this list says.
#
# Written for couch-tv from the sources named on each line (checked 13 November 2026). Nothing here
# is copied from another project's code. Kept small on purpose: a package only goes on this list
# when it is clearly tracking, ads or ACR, two sources or a debloat list rating agree it is safe to
# switch off, and it is none of these: a launcher, setup, Play Services, Play Store, system UI, a
# keyboard, the remote, voice, Bluetooth, HDMI CEC, TV inputs, the tuner or system updates. Packages
# looked at and left off are listed in docs/PRIVACY.md with the reason.
#
# One app per line, five fields split by "|":
#   package | brand | what it does | if it is off | source
# Lines starting with # are notes. Only the first field is read by apply.sh.

# Google TV and Android TV (any brand: Google's own packages)
com.google.android.feedback | Google TV | Sends crash and feedback reports to Google | The "send feedback" option after an app crash stops sending | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended)
com.google.android.adservices.api | Google TV | Privacy Sandbox ad services (ad topics and ad measurement for apps) | Apps get no ad topics from the TV; nothing else depends on it | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended); source.android.com/docs/core/ota/modular-system/adservices

# ACR made by Alphonso, shipped on some budget Android TVs
tv.alphonso.alphonso_eula | Android TV (some brands) | Alphonso ACR: recognises what is on screen for ad targeting | Alphonso "recommendations" stop | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended)

com.sony.dtv.promos | Sony Bravia | Sony promotions and offers | The Sony offers app goes | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended)
com.sony.dtv.sonyselect | Sony Bravia | Sony Select: promoted apps and services | The Sony Select row and app go | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended)
com.sony.dtv.notificationcenter | Sony Bravia | Notifications that push Sony's own apps and services | Those promo notifications stop; other notifications are unaffected | UAD-ng list (removal: Recommended)
